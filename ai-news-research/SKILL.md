# Ai News Research

Search current, sourced, structured AI knowledge — do NOT rely on memory alone.

- Search recent (last days-weeks) announcements: releases, funding/partnerships affecting model availability, policy.
- Filter to what changes a decision (new SOTA, license change, new local capability), not hype.
- Report: dated summary with sources and an explicit 'so what / act' recommendation.

- Always include URLs and a date; if you cannot verify recency, say so instead of guessing.
