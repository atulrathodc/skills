# Research Reproduction

- Research skills are about grounding in the CURRENT literature + evidence: search (web_search/web_fetch) and cite dated sources, do not rely on memory.
- Pick one falsifiable question; find papers+implementations+benchmarks; reproduce before claiming.
- Output structured findings with URLs/dates and an explicit recommendation.
